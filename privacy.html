Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2025

At Gliding Echoes Studios, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

We collect only the information you provide directly through our forms, including your name, email address, company, and any messages you choose to send us.

How We Use It

Your data is used strictly to respond to your inquiries, deliver products or services you've requested, and occasionally send relevant updates (if you've opted in).

Third-Party Services

We use trusted platforms like MailerLite (for emails) and Stripe (for payments). These third parties have their own privacy practices, which comply with global standards like GDPR and CCPA.

Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with unrelated third parties. Your information is stored securely and accessed only for the intended purpose.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from any email communication at any time using the link at the bottom of our messages.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, feel free to email us at studio@glidingechoes.com.
