Welcome to the Timecove

The Timecove is real. You might not see it right away — but it sees you.

Some kids find it when they spin in circles and fall down laughing. Others catch it in dreams, or in spirals drawn without thinking.

It hides in giggles. In treasure maps with no lines. In sunlight that feels warmer than usual.

Inside the Timecove are glowing little things — not just pebbles, but maybe shells, sparks, or stories that forgot how to speak.

No one knows how many are waiting there. Or what they’ll become once they’re found.

But if something’s been calling to you lately… if you’ve seen shapes where there weren’t any… if you’ve felt a secret waiting somewhere just out of reach…

Then maybe — just maybe — you were meant to find it first.

What Are the Pebbles?

(Whispered by a Timecove creature)

Psst... You found us. We've been waiting a long time. Not because we were lost… but because we were waiting for the right person to come looking.

The Pebbles? Oh yes — they’re here. Hundreds. Maybe more. They look like little stones… but they’re not. Not really.

They glow when you laugh. They hum when you're brave. Some even wiggle a little when they know you’re near.

Every Pebble has a secret inside — something it wants to show you. A feeling. A sparkle. A tiny truth that only comes out when it's safe.

Some Pebbles already know their names. Others are hoping you will give them one. But no matter what… once you hold one… it remembers you. And it will never, ever forget.

One Pebble Is Ready

A Pebble from the Zin season — bold and sunny and full of spark — is glowing a little brighter now. It hums when someone laughs. It wiggles when someone dares to try again, even after feeling small.

The other Pebbles are watching it now. That usually means it’s chosen someone. Someone warm. Someone kind. Someone a little bit brave.

Maybe that someone is... you.

How to Adopt Your Pebble

Bringing a Pebble home is a very big moment. It’s not just something you buy — it’s something you become part of.

When you adopt a Pebble from the Zin season, it will send you its scroll. Inside the scroll is everything it wants you to know:

  • A glowing certificate that shows you belong to each other
  • A secret trait it carries (like joy… or courage… or both)
  • A tiny whisper-message from the Pebble itself
  • And maybe even a surprise tucked in the corner

Some Pebbles already know their name. Others stay quiet, hoping you’ll give them one. You can let the Pebble choose… or you can choose for it. Either way, once your names are together on the scroll — it’s official. You’re connected. Forever.

Two Ways to Bring Your Pebble Home

1. Let Your Pebble Tell You Its Name

Some Pebbles already have a name. They’ve been keeping it safe, waiting for the right person to come along.

If you choose this path, your Pebble will tell us its name — and we’ll write it on your scroll just for you.

  • Your Pebble’s name
  • A secret it wants to share (we call it its special trait)
  • A little whisper-message
  • And maybe even a surprise from the Cove

(This path is ready fast — your Pebble’s already glowing!)

2. You Can Give Your Pebble a Name

Some Pebbles don’t have names yet. They’re waiting… hoping the right person will give them one.

If you want to choose the name yourself, you can! You’ll tell us the name, and the Timecove will write it gently into the scroll — just like magic.

  • Your Pebble’s scroll will have the name you picked
  • You’ll still get the secret trait, the whisper, and something tucked in just for you
  • It might take a little longer while the Cove finishes your custom magic

(This path is extra special. And worth the wait.)
